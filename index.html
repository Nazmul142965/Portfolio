About me

Get to know me

I'm a computer science student at Southeast University in Dhaka. I like the parts of software people don't see: a server-side clock, a record that can't be edited afterwards, a test that fails before a user does.

Most of my work is Java, Spring Boot and Go, with MongoDB, Redis and Docker around it. Beyond the backend I've trained an MRI classifier, written a 3D harbour in raw OpenGL and wired up microcontroller circuits, because it shows me how things really run.
